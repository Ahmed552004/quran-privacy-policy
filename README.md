Privacy Policy for "القرآن الكريم - تلاوات ومواقيت" App

This privacy policy applies to the "القرآن الكريم - تلاوات ومواقيت" app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices.

Information Collection and Use The Application respects your privacy and does not collect or store any personal or sensitive user data on external servers.

Location Data (GPS) The Application requests access to your device's location (Background and Foreground Location) strictly for calculating accurate Prayer Times (مواقيت الصلاة) and determining the Qibla Direction (اتجاه القبلة).

Your location data is processed locally on your device.
We do not store, share, or transmit your location data to any third-party servers.
Storage Data The Application may request storage access to download and save Quran audio files (Recitations) for offline playback. These files are stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services The Application does not use any third-party advertising or analytics services that track your behavior. It is completely ad-free.

Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
